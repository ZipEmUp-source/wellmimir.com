Help with Mimir

For Mimir on your computer and Mimir Mobile on your iPhone.

Write to us

  • In Mimir Mobile: Settings → Messages. Your message goes straight to Mimir's developers, and the answer comes back there.
  • In desktop Mimir: Settings → Account → Messages, or Report bug.
  • Without an account: open an issue on GitHub.

Common questions

How do I sign in?

Type your email and Mimir sends you a code. There is no password. The same account works on your phone and your computers.

What are Mimir credits?

Credits pay for chats with DeepSeek V4 Pro and V4 Flash at DeepSeek's own price, per token. 1,000 credits is $1. A new account gets starter credits.

Do I need a computer?

Not for chats on Mimir credits. Pair the phone with Mimir on your computer to use your agents, your files and your computer's keys from the phone.

A reply was wrong or harmful.

Press and hold the reply, then Report. It reaches the developers with the reply's words.

How do I delete my account?

In Mimir Mobile: Settings → Account → Delete my account. In desktop Mimir: Settings → Account. Everything the account holds is deleted from the service; see Privacy.
